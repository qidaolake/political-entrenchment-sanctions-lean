\section*{Declarations}

% Coauthors' confirmation of no competing interests requested 2026-10-11; pending.
\paragraph{Declaration of competing interest.} The authors declare no
competing financial interests or personal relationships that could have
influenced the work reported in this paper.

\paragraph{Funding.} This research received no specific grant from any
funding agency in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.

\paragraph{CRediT author statement.} Xiaofeng Gong:
Conceptualization, Formal analysis, Methodology, Software, Validation,
Visualization, Writing -- original draft, and Writing -- review and
editing. Yuechuan Zhao: Conceptualization, and Writing -- review and
editing. Chunping Zhong: Conceptualization, and Writing -- review and
editing.

\paragraph{Data availability.} No empirical data were used. The complete
Lean~4 formalization that machine-checks the core results of this paper, as
mapped in the formal supplement, is archived as the tagged release
\texttt{v1.30-submission} of the corresponding author's repository and accompanies this submission as supplementary material: a
source archive built from that release by
\texttt{scripts/Build-CoreBundle.ps1 -Submission}, which excludes the
third-party reference libraries and the internal staging documents. The release is
public at \url{https://github.com/qidaolake/political-entrenchment-sanctions-lean}, with the
manuscript, the Online Appendix and the formal supplement attached to it; a deposit with a DOI
will accompany publication.

\paragraph{Declaration of generative AI in the writing and formalization
process.} During the preparation of this work the authors used Claude
(Anthropic) and Codex (OpenAI) to assist the Lean~4 proof development
and the drafting and editing of the manuscript. Each core result is
independently verified by the Lean~4 kernel, passing a
\texttt{\#print axioms} check that depends only on \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}; no core result relies
on the assistants' unverified output. The authors reviewed and edited all
content and take full responsibility for it.

\paragraph{Formalization methods.} The Lean~4 development was written with
assistant-generated suggestions for proof steps and lemma search. The
selection of the statements to formalize, the economic interpretation of
every formal statement, and the correspondence map between the manuscript
and the declarations were made and reviewed by the authors, and the Lean~4
kernel independently checks every proof term in the archived release. A
kernel check certifies a formal statement; it does not certify that an
informal economic sentence translates that statement faithfully, which is
why the correspondence is recorded as a separately reviewed map in the
formal supplement.
