\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript
preparation process}
During the preparation of this work the authors used Claude (Anthropic) and Codex
(OpenAI) in order to assist the Lean~4 proof development and the drafting and editing of
the manuscript. Each core result is independently verified by the Lean~4 kernel, passing a
\texttt{\#print axioms} check that depends only on \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}; no core result relies on the
assistants' unverified output. After using these tools, the authors reviewed and edited
the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of the published
article.
