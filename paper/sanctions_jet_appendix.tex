\noindent
This appendix accompanies the manuscript \emph{Political Entrenchment and the
Persistence of Economic Sanctions}. Part~I holds the general results, which hold for
abstract payoffs and transitions; Part~II holds the parametric families and the
numerical example economies; Part~III holds the literature. In Parts~I and~II, what
the main text needs comes first and derivative material follows. Part~I opens with maps
of how the results are proved (\Cref{app:proof-maps}). The Lean~4 development and the
paper sources are public at \url{https://github.com/qidaolake/political-entrenchment-sanctions-lean}.  Results and equations are numbered locally (Lemma~A.1,
equation~(B.2), \dots).  Cross-references of the form ``Theorem~4'',
``Section~3'', or ``(12)'' point into the manuscript, not into this
document.
