\noindent
This online supplement records the exact correspondence between the
economic statements in the manuscript and the machine-checked Lean~4
development.  It is separated from the economic appendix so readers
can audit theorem statements, assumption discharges, witness
constructions, and axiom dependencies without interrupting the paper's
economic argument. Its first section reads two of the Lean statements with a table of
their notation; the second is the map. The Lean~4 development is public at
\url{https://github.com/qidaolake/political-entrenchment-sanctions-lean}.
